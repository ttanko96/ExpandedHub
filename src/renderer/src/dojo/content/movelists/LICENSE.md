# Move lists licence

The files in this folder are full move lists adapted from the **Tekken Wiki** (https://tekken.fandom.com),
by its contributors, under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Each file's
`source.title` names the wiki page it comes from; the page's history lists its authors.

**Changes made:** the moves of each page's tables were turned into the hub's command notation
(`src/shared/dojo-notation.ts`), given a kind (string, launcher, low, throw...) and hit levels; commands the
notation cannot hold are kept as text; tag moves were left out. Names, commands and hit levels are as on
the page.
